Developer name (the "Developer") values the personal information of users of EAT'ZIP. (the "App") and complies with applicable laws, including the Personal Information Protection Act of the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

1. Information stored in the App

The App is used without sign-up or login. The following information you create is stored only on your device and is not sent to the Developer's servers. The Developer cannot collect or see this information.

  • Photos and videos
  • Restaurant name, menu items, price, star rating, one-line review, dining companions, meal time, intention to revisit, tags, custom record fields, and additional notes
  • Date and time of the record, and location (coordinates and the names of the province, city, district, and neighborhood)
  • Folders, favorites, and app settings

To display records in the Home Screen widget, a small thumbnail and summary of each record are also stored in the App's shared storage on the same device. Location is not shown in the widget.

When you use the following features, the necessary information is sent to Apple's map services. This information is not passed to the Developer and is handled in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy.

  • Converting location coordinates into a place name such as "○○-gu, ○○-dong": the coordinates
  • Searching for a place on the record creation screen: the search terms you enter

5. Third-party sharing and tracking

  • We do not sell or provide personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools (SDKs), and we do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.
  • Photos and card images you send yourself through the share feature are handled under the policies of the app you choose (e.g., Messages, Instagram, KakaoTalk). Shared card images do not include location or price.

6. Deleting data

  • When you delete a record in the App, it is immediately deleted from your device along with its photos and videos, and cannot be recovered.
  • When you delete the App, all records on your device are deleted. Photos you saved separately with "Save to Photos" remain in the Photos app.
  • App data may be included in iOS device backups (iCloud or computer). Backups are managed according to Apple's policies and your settings.
  • If you would like feedback you have sent to be deleted, please contact us using the details below.
