Features

The most enjoyable way to keep a meal

Camera

Swipe to shoot

Swipe the camera button at the edge of the screen to open the camera instantly. It supports 0.5×, 1×, and telephoto zoom, flash, a rule-of-thirds grid, and a level for overhead shots.

Video

3-second videos

Steam rising from a stew, cheese stretching from a pizza: when a photo isn't enough, keep the moment as a silent 3-second clip. It plays on a loop in the record view.

Filter

Filters · Crop · Background removal

Choose from 10 filters including Vivid, Dramatic, Mono, and Noir, and crop to preset ratios. On supported devices, you can remove the background and keep just the food.

Record

Record fields that fit you

Turn on only the fields you want: restaurant name, companions, star rating, one-line review, menu, price, meal time, intention to revisit, and tags. You can also create your own fields.

Folder

Monthly folders and your own

Records are sorted into monthly folders automatically. Create folders with any name and color, like "Food Tour," and browse them as a mosaic that keeps each photo's original ratio.

Calendar

A calendar of what you ate

Each date fills with a photo of what you ate that day, so you can see a whole month of meals at a glance.

Map

A map of records by region

An illustrated map of Korea's 17 provinces and metropolitan cities shows how many records you have in each region. Location is recorded automatically from where the photo was taken, and you can correct it with place search.

Retro

Monthly and yearly recap

Your most-visited restaurant, the person you ate with most, your 5-star collection, and more. Flip through cards to look back on a month or a year of meals.

Widget

"On this day" food widget

See what you ate on this day a year ago, right on your Home Screen. The "Quick Shot" widget can also go on your Lock Screen.

How it works

Snap it, keep it, look back on it

  1. Shoot or import

    Take a photo with the camera, or pick up to 10 photos from the Photos app.

  2. Touch up

    Polish your photos with filters and cropping. You can also choose a cover photo.

  3. Record

    Write down whatever you like and tap "Archive." You can also save just the photos.

  4. Look back

    Revisit your meals through folders, the calendar, the map, search, and recaps, and share them with friends.

Privacy first

Your records stay on your device

01

No login

Install it and start right away, with no sign-up or account.

02

Nothing is uploaded to a server

Photos, videos, and record contents are all stored only on your device.

03

No ads · No tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics tools, or tracking.

04

Works without the internet

Shooting, recording, browsing, search, and recaps all work offline.

05

Only the photos you pick

The app never sees your whole photo library. Only the photos you select are passed to it.

06

Location is left out when you share

Shared images do not include location or price.
