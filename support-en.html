User guide

  1. 1

    Choose your record fields

    The first time you open the app, pick the fields you want to keep on the "What would you like to record?" screen. You can change them later, or add fields of your own, in Settings → Record Fields.

  2. 2

    Take a shot

    Swipe the camera button at the right edge of the screen all the way to the left to open the camera. Switch between photo and video mode at the top, and use the "⋯" button to turn on the flash, grid, and level. Tapping the "Quick Shot" widget also opens the camera.

  3. 3

    Touch up your photo

    Apply filters, crop to a ratio, or remove the background (on supported devices), then tap "Next." Videos go straight to the review screen. If you're not happy with the shot, tap "Retake."

  4. 4

    Save

    Choose "Add details" to write your record and save it with "Archive." Choose "Skip and save" to save just the photo right away; you can fill in the details later on the edit screen.

  5. 5

    Import photos

    Tap the "+" button in the bottom menu bar and pick up to 10 photos from the Photos app. They are added in the order you pick them, and the first one becomes the cover photo. Tap another photo on the record screen to make it the cover instead.

  6. 6

    Browse

    Move between Home (folders), Calendar, and Map from the bottom menu bar. Use the search bar on Home to find records by restaurant name, date, companions, menu, or tags.

  7. 7

    Edit · Share · Delete

    Tap "⋯" at the top right of a record card to see the favorite, share, edit, add to folder, and delete buttons. You can also delete a record by touching and holding its photo in the photo list.

Frequently asked questions

Getting started

Do I need to sign up or log in?

No. You can use the app as soon as you install it.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Shooting, recording, browsing, search, and recaps all work without the internet. Only the following features need a connection.

  • Place search
  • Converting a location into a place name such as "○○-gu, ○○-dong" (if you save offline, it is filled in automatically once you're connected again)
  • Sending feedback
I just installed the app and there are records without photos.

Those are sample records included so you can look around the app. Touch and hold them in the photo list to delete them.

The camera doesn't open when I tap the camera button.

The camera button opens by swiping it all the way to the left, not by tapping. If you turned on left-handed mode, swipe the button at the left edge to the right. You can turn left-handed mode on and off in Settings.

Photos and storage

Are the photos I take also saved to the Photos app?

Not automatically. Tap "Save to Photos" on the photo or video editing screen to save a copy to the Photos app as well.

Where are my photos stored?

Inside the EAT'ZIP. app, on your device only. They are not uploaded to a server.

Are photos stored in the app at original quality?

No. To save storage space, photos are resized to a maximum of 2048 pixels on the long side. If you need the original quality, keep a separate copy in the Photos app.

When I import photos, can the app see my whole photo library?

No. Only the photos you pick in the iOS photo picker are passed to the app, so it doesn't need permission to access your photo library.

My video has no sound.

Videos are designed to be 3 seconds long with no sound. Filters, cropping, and background removal are available for photos only.

I can't see the background removal button.

Background removal appears only on devices that support the iOS subject isolation feature.

Deleting and changing devices

How do I delete a record?

Touch and hold a photo in the photo list, or tap "⋯" → trash on a record card, then confirm.

Can I restore a deleted record?

No. A deleted record is erased immediately along with its photos and cannot be recovered. When you delete a folder, the records inside are not deleted and stay in the "All" folder.

What happens to my records if I delete the app?

All records in the app are deleted. Only photos you saved separately with "Save to Photos" remain in the Photos app.

Can I move my records to a new device?

EAT'ZIP. has no accounts or cloud sync, so records are stored only on your device. Records are included in iPhone device backups (iCloud backup or computer backup), so restoring a new device from that backup brings your records along with it. To be finalized after testing a restore on a real device

There is not yet a feature for exporting or separately backing up records from within the app.

Location and permissions

How is location recorded?

When you shoot with the camera, the place where you took the shot is recorded automatically; for imported photos, the location stored in the photo file is used. Under "Location" on the record screen, you can correct it by searching for a place or choosing from the province → city/district list.

I don't want to record my location.

Set iOS Settings → EAT'ZIP. → Location to "Never," and location won't be recorded when you take a shot. For records you've already saved, you can remove the location under "Location" on the record screen.

Turning off "Location" in Settings → Record Fields only hides the location field; the photo's location is still recorded automatically.

Is my location included when I share?

No. Shared card images do not include location or price. Location is not shown on widgets or recap cards either.

The camera screen is black.

Camera permission may be turned off. Tap the "Go to Settings" button in the app, or turn on iOS Settings → EAT'ZIP. → Camera.

"Save to Photos" isn't working.

Allow "Add Photos Only" in iOS Settings → EAT'ZIP. → Photos.

Other features

How do I add a widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of the Home Screen → tap "Edit" or "+" at the top left → find EAT'ZIP. and choose "On this day" or "Quick Shot." "Quick Shot" can also be added as a Lock Screen widget.

When can I see a recap?

A recap becomes available once you have 3 or more records in a month or a year.

Can I use the app in English?

Yes. Choose between Korean and English in Settings → Language.

Can I use it on iPad?

Yes. The app works on iPhone and iPad (iOS 17 or later), in portrait orientation only.

Good to know

  • Records are stored only on your device, so they cannot be recovered if you delete the app or lose your device. Keep device backups turned on, and save important photos to the Photos app as well.
  • Deleted records cannot be restored.
  • Photos in the app are stored at a smaller size than the originals.
  • The app supports portrait orientation only.

Still have questions?

Let us know through Settings → Send Feedback in the app, or by email.
